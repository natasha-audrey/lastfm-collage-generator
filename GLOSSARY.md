# Last.fm Collages

Collages show a Last.fm listener's top albums for a chosen listening period.

## Language

**Collage**:
A square grid of album artwork with artist and album labels, drawn from a Last.fm listener's top albums.

**Listening period**:
The span of listening history used to select a listener's top albums: seven days, one month, three months, six months, twelve months, or all time.
_Avoid_: Date range

**Grid size**:
The number of rows and columns in a collage, from three to ten; a grid size of five provides twenty-five album positions.
_Avoid_: Album count, image resolution
